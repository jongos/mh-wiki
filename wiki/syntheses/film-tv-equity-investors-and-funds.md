# Film and TV Equity Investors and Film Funds

Match the project to the investor’s mandate before presenting recoupment terms. This selection separates investment managers and documentary equity from studios that finance their own projects or partnerships. It is not a ranking, and a studio’s financing activity does not establish an open equity fund.

## Investor Access Is a Separate Role

The existing [[wiki/syntheses/finance-and-ip-directory|Finance and IP Partner Directory]] includes Slated and Sundance Catalyst as investor-access routes. Neither an introduction nor participation in a marketplace means an investor has committed equity.

## Prepare an Equity Inquiry

Provide rights ownership, script or documentary access, budget, team, finance already secured, sales/distribution strategy and proposed [[wiki/concepts/equity-financing-and-recoupment|Recoupment Terms]]. Specify project versus slate investment, preferred return, expenses deducted before recoupment, approvals and profit participation.

## Analysis and Inference

The fund and strategic-studio categories are editorial classifications of the linked public descriptions. Confirm the actual investment instrument and whether unsolicited material is accepted before sending a script. Do not infer current fund size, available investment capital or expected returns from past films.
