## Prepare a Coverage Inquiry

Supply territories, dates, budget, cast, equipment, stunts, vehicles, locations, employment arrangements, rights-clearance status and delivery requirements. Ask for the carrier, broker, policy wording, exclusions, deductibles, limits, endorsements and claims route. General customer-service contacts above are not claims-notification instructions.

## Analysis and Inference

A broker arranges coverage; an insurer or subscribing carriers bear the defined risk. The group or platform name alone does not identify the issuer. Compare completion obligations separately in the [[wiki/syntheses/completion-bond-companies|Completion Bond Directory]] and [[wiki/concepts/protection-stack|Protection Stack]].
