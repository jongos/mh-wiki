# Film Finance and IP Partner Directory

Choose a partner by the capital or commercial role the project needs. This directory separates direct investment, access to investors, grants, public support and IP licensing. Check the linked program’s current intake before preparing an application.

Category directories linked below list lenders, insurers, completion guarantors and additional funders with per-entry **Last checked** dates and, where sources publish them, comparable award or deal-size columns.

## How We Verified Who Is Listed

A row appears here when a retained research record shows the organization’s **official website or program page** publicly describes the relevant role and a business contact or application route. Third-party directories alone do not verify a provider; they are listed only on [[wiki/syntheses/film-finance-external-directories|External Funding Directories]], with a separate review note.

Reviews use public pages only. Contacts are not tested by outreach. Figures in comparison columns are copied only when the cited page publishes them; nothing is estimated.

Each table’s **Last checked** date comes from the newest applicable research record for that entry (individual program reviews or the November 18, 2026 category contact reviews). **Unchecked** would mean no retained check date; this hub does not add unaudited names. Category pages are refreshed on a rolling basis when programs change; stale rows can be updated without re-dating every other entry on the page.

To report a wrong link, role label or contact, email [info@mediahedge.com](mailto:info@mediahedge.com) with the page title, row name and the source you believe is correct.

## Analysis and Inference

Shortlist providers by format, stage, geography, rights and funding type. Prepare the [[wiki/syntheses/producer-financing-route|Producer Financing Package]] before outreach. Record the exact legal counterparty and its written terms; a directory listing establishes the role described on the linked page, not a transaction commitment.
