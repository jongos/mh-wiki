# Film and TV Grants and Public Funding Providers

Choose by format, production stage and applicant geography. These selected programs include grants and other public or nonprofit funding; their instruments are not interchangeable. Review [[wiki/concepts/grants-and-soft-money|Grants and Soft Money]] before treating an award as nonrecoupable production cash.

## Intake and Funding-Type Checks

As reviewed November 18, 2026, [ITVS](https://itvs.org/get-funded/) announces an Independent Lens Production Call opening January 4, 2027 at 9 a.m. Pacific; its Incubator and Open Call routes are marked closed. This updates the older directory’s general closed-round description. Recheck the program page before preparing an application.

The BFI and Telefilm entries are public-funding routes, not promises of unrestricted grants. Confirm applicant eligibility, recoupment, rights, match funding, milestones and eligible costs in the applicable guidelines and agreement. Fiscal sponsorship is a separate fundraising service; see the [[wiki/syntheses/finance-and-ip-directory|Partner Directory]].

## Find Additional Programs

Use [[wiki/syntheses/film-finance-external-directories|External Funding Directories]] for more territories, subjects and stages. For credits and rebates, use [[wiki/syntheses/us-film-tax-incentives|U.S. Production Incentives]] rather than classifying them as grants.

## Analysis and Inference

A program’s existence is not evidence that its intake is open or that a project qualifies. This directory records contact and program scope; it does not replace the current application documents.
