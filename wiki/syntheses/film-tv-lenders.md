# Film and TV Finance Lenders

Start with the repayment asset and the amount and timing of cash needed. These selected, established providers publicly describe film or television lending. The list emphasizes U.S. and UK project finance and is not a ranking by lending volume.

## Prepare a Lending Inquiry

Supply the budget, finance plan, production schedule, requested facility, repayment contracts, incentive status, rights position and delivery plan. Separate contracted receivables from unsold-rights estimates. Compare [[wiki/concepts/pre-sales-collateral|Pre-Sales]], [[wiki/concepts/tax-credit-collateral|Tax-Credit Collateral]] and [[wiki/concepts/gap-collateral|Gap Collateral]] before asking for terms.

## Analysis and Inference

Selection reflects publicly documented specialist capability and established industry presence, not independently measured market share. A service menu does not establish available capacity, approval or the legal lender for a particular transaction.
