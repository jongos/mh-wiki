# A Producer’s Guide to Financing

Build the package around three questions: what will the production cost, who supplies the cash, and how will each funder receive its agreed return? Keep the creative package, commercial evidence and financial schedules aligned as the project develops.

## Develop and Package the Project

Establish the producer and production entity, script or documentary treatment, director, key attachments and [[wiki/concepts/chain-of-title-and-rights|Rights Record]]. Explain the audience, release strategy and relevant comparable projects. Identify which attachments are signed, conditional or still proposed.

For early development, separate money needed to build the package from production capital. Options, script work, budgeting, casting and sales preparation consume cash before the project can meet a lender’s closing conditions. [Sundance Catalyst](https://www.sundance.org/initiatives/catalyst/) provides a practical example of preparation for independent investors.

## Reconcile the Three Financial Documents

| Document | Question It Answers | Required Connection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget | What will production and delivery cost? | Include post, clearances, insurance, financing, contingency and delivery |
| Finance plan | Who supplies each contribution? | Separate cash, debt, equity, grants, deferrals and in-kind support |
| Cash-flow schedule | When can the production spend it? | Match draw conditions and funding dates to payroll, vendors and completion |

Use [[wiki/concepts/full-financing|Full Financing]] and the [[wiki/syntheses/film-finance-worked-example|Downloadable Worked Model]] to reconcile gross sources, deductions and usable cash.

## Match the Project to Capital

Approach [[wiki/concepts/equity-financing-and-recoupment|Equity Investors]] with rights, recoupment terms and downside economics. Approach lenders with evidenced repayment assets and completion funding. Match [[wiki/concepts/grants-and-soft-money|Grants and Public Support]] to geography, format, stage and eligible applicant. Use the [[wiki/syntheses/finance-and-ip-directory|Finance and IP Directory]] to identify the relevant route.

## Organize the Data Room

Keep a dated index with current versions of company records, rights agreements, budget, schedule, finance plan, weekly cash flow, bankable contracts, incentive evidence, insurance/completion proposals, investor terms and delivery requirements. Provide a conditions list showing each missing approval or document, its owner and due date. Restrict access to personal and confidential records.

## Close Before Committing the Spend

Reconcile signed commitments to funding conditions and the closing statement. Confirm equity funding order, account instructions, lien and consent requirements, [[wiki/concepts/completion-protection|Completion Protection]] and the overage plan. An introduction, expression of interest or approved application does not establish cleared funds. Start each draw with evidence that the required conditions are satisfied.

## Analysis and Inference

Run a financing delay case before setting the production start date. If a late draw creates negative cash, secure a documented bridge, change the spending schedule or close the funding gap before production proceeds. Keep distribution deductions and recoupment terms visible to everyone funding the project.
