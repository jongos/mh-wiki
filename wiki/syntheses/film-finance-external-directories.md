# Film Finance and Funding Directories

Use these other lists to broaden a financing search, then verify each shortlisted organization on its own website. Directory classifications, entry counts and application dates can lag the provider’s current offering.

## Providers and Public Contacts

Public sources checked November 18, 2026. Contact details are published business routes; no inquiry has been sent. The source links in each row support its role and contact information.

## Verify Before Importing a Listing

Check the official provider’s current role, application policy and contact route. Commercial directories can mix debt, equity, grants, public investment and intermediaries in one list. The FILM.FUND directory, for example, groups several different types of capital provider; its category labels are not adopted as underwriting classifications here.

## Analysis and Inference

These lists are discovery resources, not independent corroboration of a provider’s capacity or an offer to finance a project. The curated category pages use provider or program sources for substantive role and contact information; the Production Guild contact is explicitly association-sourced.
