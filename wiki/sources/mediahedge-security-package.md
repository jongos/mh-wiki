# MediaHedge Security Package

## Scope

Financier brief describing how security grants, perfection, collateral assignments, counterparty acknowledgments, account control, powers of attorney and interparty documents make film-finance collateral reachable.

## Key claims

- Credit analysis identifies value; the security package determines whether the financier can reach it.
- A generic all-assets lien may be necessary but insufficient for receivables, incentive rights, copyrights, accounts and contracts across entities and jurisdictions.
- Each asset requires the correct attachment, perfection, control or recordation method.
- Counterparty notices and acknowledgments can protect payment, notice, cure and amendment rights.
- Roles such as lender, purchaser, participant, agent and collateral agent are not interchangeable.

## Controls and decision points

- reconcile each collateral asset, owner, obligor, jurisdiction and proceeds path;
- review lien searches, releases, filings, control agreements, recordation and opinions;
- test consent, anti-assignment, setoff, termination, notice and cure terms;
- allocate enforcement, voting, expense and recovery rights among financing parties;
- preserve materials access, delegated powers, custody and replacement mechanics.

## Limits

A UCC filing does not prove ownership, attachment, universal priority or deposit-account control. Copyright, foreign and government-receivable rules may require separate steps. Statutory and stakeholder claims can alter practical priority. Enforcement value can still depend on completion and delivery.
