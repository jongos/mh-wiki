# Monitoring and Servicing After Closing

## Scope

Financier brief describing post-close surveillance of production, collateral, cash, covenants and investor reporting. Its central claim is that servicing should enable timely decisions rather than merely produce reports.

## Key claims

- Film-finance risk and collateral form change after closing.
- A current baseline must connect budget, schedule, borrowing base, collateral calendar and expected cash curve.
- Every exception should have severity, owner, cure date and quantified effect on value, timing and return.
- Trigger thresholds should connect variance to draw, reserve, waiver, claim, reforecast or workout decisions.
- Custody, data portability and backup servicing protect investors from operational dependency.

## Controls and decision points

- production cost-to-complete and schedule surveillance;
- dynamic collateral eligibility, obligor, incentive and concentration review;
- contract-to-bank-to-subledger cash reconciliation;
- covenant, waiver, amendment and expiration logs;
- investor consent thresholds and reserved matters;
- standardized exposure, accrual, maturity and recovery reporting.

## Limits

Reporting volume can conceal stale or unverified data. Percentage complete may differ from cash spent. Aging may hide delivery disputes. Accrued interest may mask deteriorating XIRR. Informal waivers can prejudice remedies. The brief does not state the required cadence or severity thresholds.
