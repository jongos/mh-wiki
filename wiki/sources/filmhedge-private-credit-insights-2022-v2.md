# FilmHedge Private Credit Insights 2022 - Revised Edition

## Scope and Authority

Historical production budgets, cost reconciliation, Georgia activity and lending observations. Revised September 2, 2026; the research period remains 2022. 28 PDF pages. Local publisher report from the owner's Reports collection; its title, edition and page count agree with the live [report landing page](https://filmhedge.com/report-2022-private-credit-insights) inspected October 7, 2026. Public download bytes were not independently compared. This is publisher research, not audited performance or current approved underwriting policy.

## Reader-Suitable Material

Pages 6, 9 and 12-14 distinguish the ten-transaction sample from the application pipeline and explain cost-table reconciliation. Pages 17 and 19-25 separate historical activity, illustrative impact, pricing and return measures.

## Excluded Claims and Limits

The cost totals exceed reported budgets for three productions, and the Buddies BTL figure conflicts across original tables. Unexplained residuals are not contingency. Lending groups overlap; transaction weights and net-return conventions are missing. Benchmark dates and tenors are not comparable. No current rates, representative budget allocation, market share, causal incentive effect or portfolio outperformance is established.
