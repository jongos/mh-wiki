## Authority and Scope

[Official source](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/offering-pathways). Official small-business offering overview, updated April 24, 2026. Securities offerings must use registration or an applicable exemption. Solicitation, investor eligibility and intermediary requirements depend on the chosen route.
