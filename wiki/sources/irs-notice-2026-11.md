# IRS Notice 2026-11

## Scope, Authority and Review

IRS interim guidance, published in Internal Revenue Bulletin 2026-06. Review is scoped to Notice 2026-11 (sections 1–8), not the bulletin’s unrelated items. It explains acquisition timing, January 2025 substitutions, elections and conditional reliance pending regulations; it does not create a film credit.

[Official source](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2026-06_IRB). Retrieved November 4, 2026 without changing source bytes. Retrieval and page-update dates are not effective dates. Review scope is federal film/TV draft guidance, not a taxpayer opinion.

## Suitable Claims and Exclusions

Suitable for reader publication: the scoped statutory/guidance explanation or expressly contingent proposal described above, retaining dates, qualifications and source links. Excluded: guaranteed taxpayer eligibility, tax savings, financing approval, assumed enactment, unsupported transfer pricing and individualized tax advice. The reviewed reader explanation is published separately; retained drafts remain unpublished.
