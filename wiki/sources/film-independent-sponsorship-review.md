# Film Independent Fiscal Sponsorship — Review

## Authority and Scope

[Official source](https://www.filmindependent.org/programs/fiscal-sponsorship/). Official fiscal sponsorship page. Sponsorship enables eligible projects to receive qualifying charitable donations and pursue grants through nonprofit administration. The page states new applications pause October 26, 2026 and resume January 11, 2027.

## Reader Use and Exclusions

Sponsorship is not a grant award or investment commitment; individual donation deductibility is not determined here.
