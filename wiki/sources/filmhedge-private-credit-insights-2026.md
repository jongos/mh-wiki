# FilmHedge Private Credit Insights 2026

## Scope and Authority

Reader-oriented companion to Divergent Correlation, covering debt versus equity, production finance plans, audience data and credit measurement. Published September 3, 2026, v1. 29 PDF pages. Local publisher report from the owner's Reports collection; its title, edition and page count agree with the live [report landing page](https://filmhedge.com/report-2026-private-credit-insights) inspected October 7, 2026. Public download bytes were not independently compared. This is publisher research, not audited performance or current approved underwriting policy.

## Reader-Suitable Material

Pages 5 and 22-24 frame the practical questions: payer, contract, conditions, control, timing and collateral slices. Pages 10-12 reuse the technical study cohorts; page 28 identifies the companion study as the statistical source.

## Excluded Claims and Limits

The same cohort is not a second independent study. Page 16 asserts a strong grading/performance relationship and page 17 uses uncorrelated language beyond the technical study conclusion; page 26 includes cast and genre in its conclusion although page 13 says star power was not coded. The wiki uses the technical paper for empirical scope and excludes these broader assertions. Page 11 calls mean loan size funded while page 12 defines underwritten principal; preserve the latter measure. Public guide-pricing and counterparty snapshots are not imported as current policy. Film stills and celebrity images are excluded.
