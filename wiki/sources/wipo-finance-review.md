# WIPO Rights, Camera, Action — Financing Review

## Authority and Scope

[Official source](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/pubdocs/en/wipo-pub-869-22-en-rights-camera-action-intellectual-property-rights-and-the-filmmaking-process.pdf). 2022 edition; chapters 1, 2 and 5, scoped to financing, distribution, television and coproduction. Equity participation, contractual deductions, television deficit/cost-plus structures and official coproduction approval are distinct mechanisms. Commercial terms depend on the agreement.
