# FilmHedge Divergent Correlation 2026

## Scope and Authority

Exploratory statistical study of a 34-project funded sample with question-specific cohorts. Analysis date September 1, 2026; publication September 3, 2026. 26 PDF pages. Local publisher report from the owner's Reports collection; its title, edition and page count agree with the live [report landing page](https://filmhedge.com/report-2026-divergent-correlation) inspected October 7, 2026. Public download bytes were not independently compared. This is publisher research, not audited performance or current approved underwriting policy.

## Reader-Suitable Material

Pages 5-9 define 34 study projects, 22 observed payoffs, 8 active and 4 undetermined; public gross is observed for 19, with 15 in the joint payoff/gross cohort. Pages 10-21 and appendices show broad uncertainty, vintage confounding, model instability and requirements for prospective validation.

## Excluded Claims and Limits

Underwritten principal is not a verified aggregate of cash disbursed. Public theatrical gross is not lender cash recovery or total film revenue. No stable causal or predictive underwriting relationship, default-probability calibration, blended-loan advantage or higher-LTV policy follows. Zero coded resolved defaults is a sample condition, not a portfolio-wide performance claim. The companion Insights publication uses this same study and is not independent corroboration.
