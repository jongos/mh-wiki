# IRS Publication 925 — Loss-Limitation Review

## Authority and Scope

[Official source](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p925). Official 2025 Publication 925, scoped to ordering and application of basis, at-risk, passive-activity and excess-business-loss limitations. A deduction may not be usable against current income; a passive film investment does not automatically offset wages.

## Reader Use and Exclusions

Not an individual taxpayer determination; filing-year law and entity facts govern. Review date is not the publication effective date.
