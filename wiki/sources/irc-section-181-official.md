# Official IRC Section 181 Snapshot

## Scope, Authority and Review

Enacted statutory text; scope includes production eligibility, compensation test, episode rule, election/cost limits, no double deduction and termination for productions commencing after December 31, 2025. Retrieved currency is October 7, 2026; this does not independently date-check all later legislation.

[Official source](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-118/t26/s181/). Retrieved November 4, 2026 without changing source bytes. Retrieval and page-update dates are not effective dates. Review scope is federal film/TV draft guidance, not a taxpayer opinion.

## Suitable Claims and Exclusions

Suitable for reader publication: the scoped statutory/guidance explanation or expressly contingent proposal described above, retaining dates, qualifications and source links.
