# IRS Publication 946 — Special Depreciation Review

## Authority and Scope

[Official source](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946). Official 2025 Publication 946, chapter 3, scoped to special depreciation allowance, basis reduction and potential ordinary-income recapture on disposition.

## Reader Use and Exclusions

Do not infer that ordinary MACRS rules apply to films or that initial expensing eliminates later taxable income. Taxpayer-specific treatment is not established.
