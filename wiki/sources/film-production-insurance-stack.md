# Film-Production Insurance Stack

## Scope

Financier brief mapping production-package, liability, workforce, E&O, specialty and lender-related policy protections. It distinguishes risk-specific insurance from completion, receivables-default and repayment protection.

## Key claims

- A production combines cast, physical, liability, workforce, digital, IP and contractual risks that require different policies.
- Insurance preserves budget or collateral value only after a covered event within policy terms.
- Certificates are evidence, not the operative policy or endorsement.
- Loss-payee and additional-insured status address different interests and do not automatically confer all notice or proceeds rights.
- E&O complements but does not replace chain-of-title and clearance diligence.

## Controls and decision points

- map activities, assets, personnel, locations and delivery duties to policies;
- review actual policies, endorsements, limits, deductibles, sublimits, exclusions and carrier strength;
- verify premium payment, policy periods and claims-made continuity where applicable;
- align financier status and proceeds use with the security and completion documents;
- preserve claim notice, mitigation, documentation and proof-of-loss procedures.

## Limits

Exclusions, warranties, late notice, unpaid premiums, aggregation, policy periods and coverage-specific limits can defeat recovery. Insurance does not ordinarily protect against distributor solvency, tax-credit realization, unsold-rights performance or general loan default.
