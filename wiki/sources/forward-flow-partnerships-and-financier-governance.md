# Forward-Flow Partnerships and Financier Governance

## Scope

Financier brief describing how a capital partner and MediaHedge allocate investment policy, funding, ownership, representations, servicing, exceptions, remedies and continuity in a scaled forward-flow relationship.

## Key claims

- Routine eligible assets should close efficiently without sacrificing investor control over material risk.
- The buy-box must be objective enough to make funding obligations and exceptions auditable.
- Ownership, title transfer, agency, perfection, custody and segregated cash must fit the intended legal structure.
- Repurchase should address defined origination defects and should not silently become full credit recourse.
- Data, audit, custody, backup servicing and replacement rights preserve portfolio operability beyond the originator.

## Controls and decision points

- standardized underwriting package and eligibility certificate;
- approval timing and settlement mechanics;
- asset-level representations with tailored cure, indemnity or repurchase;
- delegated servicing authority with measurable standards;
- investor consent for exceptions, concentrations, extensions, compromises, releases, enforcement and conflicts;
- termination, replacement and run-off procedures.

## Limits

Subjective criteria can make a commitment optional; rigid criteria can impair deployment. True sale, participation, lending, agency and risk-retention consequences differ. Fee conflicts, allocation, confidentiality, cyber risk, concentration calculations and servicer-termination economics need explicit treatment.
