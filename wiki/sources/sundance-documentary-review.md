## Authority and Scope

[Official source](https://www.sundance.org/programs/documentary-film/). Official Documentary Film Program page. The fund offers nonrecoupable support for independent cinematic documentaries from development through post-production. Work samples and stage requirements apply; solely story-research proposals are distinguished from supported development.
