# Portfolio Construction and Concentration Risk

## Scope

Financier brief extending loan-level underwriting into factor-based portfolio construction, concentration limits, maturity liquidity, exception governance and performance attribution.

## Key claims

- Deal count, production title and borrower SPV count do not establish diversification.
- Common failure drivers include obligors, incentive jurisdictions, completion guarantors, sales agents, producers, platforms, banks, collateral markets and collection windows.
- Exposure should include current funded principal, committed but undrawn amounts and stressed loss contribution.
- Each new loan should be assessed for incremental portfolio impact as well as standalone quality.
- Exceptions consume a finite risk budget and should remain attributable to the risk factor and approving authority.

## Controls and decision points

- stable identifiers for counterparties, affiliates, collateral, jurisdictions, producers and risk factors;
- hard limits, approval thresholds and watch levels with clear calculation and cure rules;
- maturity ladders, draw utilization and commitment-coverage forecasts;
- common-shock stress tests and correlated downside overlays;
- reporting of top exposures, headroom, watch-list migration, weighted advance rates, expected loss and realized return.

## Limits

Different collateral categories can share the same completion or delivery event. Guarantees or insurance should reduce exposure only when enforceable, collectible and not concentrated. Low historical loss may reflect a small sample, favorable vintage or unresolved extensions. Market-value collateral may become least liquid during common stress.
