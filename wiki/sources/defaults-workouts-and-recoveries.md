# Defaults, Workouts and Recoveries

## Scope

Financier brief defining a five-stage response to production, collateral and payment deterioration. It frames a workout as an economic value-preservation process rather than an automatic concession or immediate foreclosure.

## Key claims

- Rapid diagnosis must distinguish liquidity, documentation, performance, obligor-credit and fundamental collateral problems.
- Early action preserves completion, cure, claim and enforcement options.
- Remedy selection should compare risk-adjusted net present recovery, including additional funding, cost, delay, collateral decay and execution probability.
- Protective advances and extensions require explicit value and governance tests.
- Recovery reporting should separate gross collections, expenses, nonaccrual, charge-offs and net realized recovery.

## Controls and decision points

- triage facts, suspend uncontrolled activity and issue reservations;
- stabilize cash, contracts, materials, insurance, filings and production liquidity;
- preserve claim, notice, cure, limitation and termination deadlines;
- compare cure, takeover, extension, sale, enforcement and restructuring paths;
- obtain required investor approval for advances, modifications, releases, settlements and enforcement;
- route recoveries through controlled accounts and the agreed waterfall.

## Limits

Cross-defaults and claim deadlines may move faster than investigation. Completion guarantors may elect takeover and dispute coverage. UCC remedies require commercially reasonable process. Protective advances, repeated extensions and foreign or intellectual-property enforcement can increase loss rather than preserve value.
