# MediaHedge Protection Stack

## Scope

Financier brief synthesizing the layered controls used to protect capital across underwriting, sizing, legal priority, cash control, completion, insurance, monitoring and enforcement.

## Key claims

- Film-finance risks are distinct: production, delivery, obligor, documentation and cash-diversion risk require different controls.
- Eligible value must be independently supported before entering the borrowing base.
- Conservative exposure and priority provide structural protection before pricing.
- Completion and insurance instruments receive credit only for the risks their terms cover.
- Monitoring should trigger action while several recovery options remain.

## Controls and decision points

- verify each asset, obligor, condition, deduction and collection date;
- apply the tightest advance, LTV, concentration, budget, reserve and tenor test;
- align security, assignments, notices, accounts, completion and insurance documents;
- establish reporting, triggers, claims, authority and enforcement playbooks at closing;
- preserve capital-partner rights to information, audit, waiver, amendment and enforcement decisions.

## Limits

Layering does not eliminate risk. Duplicate documents may not create independent recovery. Unperfected liens, uncontrolled accounts, exclusions, obligor defenses and inconsistent notices create gaps. Pricing cannot compensate for ineligible collateral, and correlation can cause several layers to fail together.
