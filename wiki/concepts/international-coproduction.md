# International Coproduction

An international coproduction combines producers’ money, services, creative work or market access across countries. The agreement must connect those contributions to ownership, decision rights, delivery and recoupment. Official treaty or convention status follows a separate approval process.

## Separate the Commercial Deal from Official Status

A private coproduction agreement does not itself confer national status or program eligibility. Map the applicable treaty, competent authorities, application timing, financial and creative participation, local expenditure and final certification. Each incentive or public fund then applies its own eligibility rules. [WIPO’s guide](https://www.wipo.int/publications/en/details.jsp?id=4611&plang=EN), chapter 5, distinguishes contractual and official coproduction structures.

## Reconcile Contributions and Rights

| Schedule | Required Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Contributions | Cash, services, currency, payment date, conditions and responsibility for shortfalls |
| Production responsibilities | Who hires, spends, insures, reports and delivers each component |
| Ownership and exploitation | Copyright shares, territories, reserved rights, sales authority and approval rights |
| Public support | Applicant, approved costs, award terms, recoupment and certification dependencies |
| Collections | Territorial receipts, account routing, expenses, investor priorities and reporting |
| Disputes and continuity | Governing law, replacement, default, step-in and completion responsibilities |

## Compare Net Location Economics

Compare the total delivered cost after eligible support, additional travel, local requirements, professional fees, currency conversion and financing time. A higher headline incentive can leave less usable cash after exclusions and delays. Link the incentive calculation to [[wiki/concepts/tax-credit-collateral|Collateral Diligence]] and the [[wiki/concepts/full-financing|Production Cash Schedule]].

Record the exchange rate and currency of every commitment and material use. Show the unhedged mismatch and the cost, settlement date and counterparty of any hedge. A budget conversion rate alone does not fix the cash conversion rate.

## Analysis and Inference

Review the financing as one production even when each partner prepares a local budget. Eliminate intercompany transfers and duplicate support before totaling sources. Check that territorial carve-outs in the coproduction agreement match the [[wiki/concepts/chain-of-title-and-rights|Rights Schedule]] and lender assignments. Use [[wiki/syntheses/finance-and-ip-directory|Official Funding Routes]] for current application documents; [Telefilm’s Production Program](https://telefilm.ca/en/financing/production) provides finance-plan and budget templates for its own applicants.
