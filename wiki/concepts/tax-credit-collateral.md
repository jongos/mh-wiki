# Tax-Credit Collateral

A production incentive can support a bridge loan when the project, claimant and expenditures qualify under the governing program and the expected refund, rebate, grant or transferable certificate can be assigned or otherwise captured for the lender. The expected incentive is conditional value until program, audit, filing and realization requirements are satisfied.

## Underwriting Architecture

### Eligibility

Program counsel and incentive specialists confirm the jurisdiction, claimant entity, project type, expenditure categories, residency or local-hire tests, caps and transfer rules. The analysis must distinguish refundable credits, rebates, grants and transferable certificates because their ownership and enforcement mechanics differ.

### Verified Spend and Valuation

The expected amount is rebuilt from governing rules, the production budget, qualified-spend schedule, payroll, vendor evidence and professional estimate. Nonqualifying costs, fees, caps, holdbacks, transfer discounts, timing risk and recapture exposure reduce the collectible basis.

### Advance and Structure

The tax-credit brief states a current MediaHedge cap of 85% of verified eligible value, subject to tighter transaction constraints. Security, notices, claimant ownership, lockbox or account control and certificate-transfer documentation must fit local anti-assignment and program rules.

### Realization

Cost reports track qualified spend during production. After completion, required audits, applications and certificates are submitted. Refund proceeds or certificate-sale proceeds flow to the controlled account and contractual waterfall.

## Principal Risks

- nonqualifying or migrated spend;
- missed residency, local-hire, filing or long-stop requirements;
- audit reductions, processing delays or program changes;
- transfer discount, buyer settlement and liquidity risk;
- claimant tax liabilities, government setoff, fraud or recapture;
- statutory anti-assignment and imperfect payment capture;
- timing beyond loan maturity.

An estimate is not an unconditional government receivable, and a completion guaranty ordinarily does not reimburse an incentive shortfall.

## Monitoring Evidence

Track eligible spend versus budget, cost reports, payroll and vendor support, filing milestones, audit status, certificate issuance, buyer settlement, expected-versus-actual timing and any change in program or claimant standing. Changes must update [[wiki/concepts/loan-sizing|the borrowing base]] and [[wiki/concepts/monitoring-and-servicing|the collection forecast]].
