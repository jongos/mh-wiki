# Gap Collateral

Gap financing advances against a discounted portion of the potential value of verified unsold film or television rights before binding licenses exist. The asset is market-dependent rights and future sale proceeds—not cash, a guaranteed sale or a contracted minimum-guarantee receivable.

## The Collateral Structure

1. **Verified unsold rights:** a rights schedule identifies available territories, media, languages, windows, restrictions and liens.
2. **Supported low estimate:** a credible sales agent provides territory cases grounded in comparable sales, buyer demand and current market conditions.
3. **Conservative advance:** unsupported, duplicative, stale, unavailable and encumbered value is excluded before every [[wiki/concepts/loan-sizing|sizing limit]] is applied.
4. **Completion and sales execution:** [[wiki/concepts/completion-protection|Completion Protection]] supports delivery while the sales arrangement governs marketing, reporting and license execution.
5. **Proceeds control:** the [[wiki/concepts/security-package|Security Package]] covers the rights, future licenses and proceeds, which flow through [[wiki/concepts/cash-control-and-waterfalls|Cash Control and Waterfalls]].

## From Estimate to Receivable

| State | What Exists | Principal Risk | Primary Control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Unsold rights | Available rights plus an estimate of possible value | Ownership, market demand, valuation, correlation and timing | Rights schedule, chain of title, low-case support and conservative advance |
| Executed license | Identified distributor and contractual minimum guarantee | Delivery, acceptance, obligor credit, defenses and deductions | [[wiki/concepts/pre-sales-collateral\|Pre-Sales Diligence]], assignment and acknowledgment |
| Collected cash | Cleared proceeds in the controlled account | Diversion, priority, deductions and reconciliation | [[wiki/concepts/cash-control-and-waterfalls\|Cash Control and Waterfalls]] |

## Current Policy Application

Gap exposure is subject to approved transaction requirements and any applicable funding mandate. Underwriting applies the current asset-specific advance rule to a supported low case, tests the result against concentration-review requirements, and then applies transaction-specific leverage, budget-exposure, term and liquidity requirements. A concentration-review trigger requires explicit human analysis rather than automatic resizing or decline. Unsupported or highly correlated value receives no credit merely because another ratio has remaining capacity.

Gap remains excluded from the current institutional segment even when another portion of the same production is eligible. Other funding arrangements require their own approval. Gap can affect transaction pricing, but there is no mechanical fixed uplift: the complete collateral mix, timing, protection and risk analysis inform the recommendation. Cash timing, extensions, losses, [[wiki/concepts/monitoring-and-servicing|servicing]] and actual loan-tape performance determine [[wiki/concepts/financier-return-economics|realized economics]].

## Diligence Questions

- Does the borrower own the rights, and are the identified territories and media genuinely available?
- Are the sales agent's authority, experience, comparable titles, buyer evidence and actual record supportable?
- Does the low case reflect commissions, taxes, currency, timing, overlap and realistic collection costs?
- Which sizing rail binds after stress, and does the loan remain [[wiki/concepts/full-financing|fully financed]]?
- Are future licenses, notices, proceeds and account directions captured by the security and cash-control structure?
- How much [[wiki/concepts/portfolio-construction|portfolio exposure]] shares the same sales agent, genre, buyer market, delivery window or territory assumptions?

## Limits and Evidence

A sales estimate is an opinion of potential value. It can fail because of weak demand, delayed delivery, rights conflicts, optimistic comparables, currency, tax, commissions or expenses. Territory values may be correlated, and completion protection does not ordinarily pay because unsold rights underperform. Pricing cannot cure unsupported value, concentration or a broken repayment timeline.

## External Context

Screen Australia's current [Market and Audience Insights](https://www.screenaustralia.gov.au/market-audience-insights/) reviewed distribution advances, minimum guarantees, pre-sales and gap across 197 feature-film and television applications submitted from January 2023 through October 2025. The agency describes marketplace funding as important but rarely sufficient by itself. Its official [Finance Plans Explained](https://www.screenaustralia.gov.au/podcast-finance-plans-explained/) guide distinguishes pre-sales from gap loans based on reputable sales-agent estimates for unsold territories. These sources support the conceptual distinction and the need for layered financing; they do not validate a MediaHedge estimate, advance rate, concentration limit or price.
