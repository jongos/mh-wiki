# Grants, Fiscal Sponsorship and Soft Money

Soft money is an industry label for several kinds of support with different obligations. Identify the actual instrument before counting it in the [[wiki/concepts/full-financing|Finance Plan]]: a grant, recoupable public investment, tax incentive, donation or service contribution.

## Classify the Contribution

| Funding Type | What It Provides | Terms to Track |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grant | An award for approved activity | Applicant, eligible costs, milestones, reporting, repayment triggers and unused funds |
| Public investment | Production capital with specified recoupment or rights | Waterfall position, approvals, producer entitlements and reuse restrictions |
| Tax incentive | A statutory benefit under a particular program | Qualification, award/certificate, net proceeds and collection timing |
| Fiscal sponsorship | Administration through a nonprofit for qualifying charitable funding | Mission fit, sponsor oversight, fees, disbursement and donor restrictions |
| In-kind contribution | An identified service or item | Supported value, scope, availability and cash replacement cost |

[Film Independent](https://www.filmindependent.org/programs/fiscal-sponsorship/) explains how fiscal sponsorship can provide access to grants and charitable donations. The sponsor relationship itself is not a grant award. Keep donations distinct from investments that promise financial participation.

## Build the Funding Calendar

Record opening and decision dates, prerequisites, agreement execution, payment milestones and holdbacks. Test whether an award pays during development, after expenditure or on delivery. Include sponsor charges, reporting costs and any required match in the net cash schedule. Count an awarded source only when its conditions and timing support the intended use.

## Finance a Documentary in Stages

Separate development, production, post-production and distribution needs. A development grant can fund research or a sample without financing the full film. Update the budget as the story and archive requirements evolve. If later equity joins a grant-supported project, specify the recoupable capital and investor participation rather than treating all budget funding as investor capital. [Sundance’s Documentary Film Program](https://www.sundance.org/programs/documentary-film/) and [Catalyst](https://www.sundance.org/initiatives/catalyst/) illustrate these distinct funding routes.

## Public Support and Producer Rights

Public funding can carry recoupment, copyright and approval provisions. The [BFI Locked Box](https://www.bfi.org.uk/get-funding-support/bfi-locked-box) illustrates how particular producer entitlements can be reserved for future eligible activity. Apply the named program’s agreement rather than assuming every public contribution is nonrecoupable or freely distributable.

## Analysis and Inference

Keep one cost allocation across funders. Do not charge the same expense twice where award terms prohibit it, or count donated equipment as cash for payroll.
