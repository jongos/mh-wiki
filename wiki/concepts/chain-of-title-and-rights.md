# Chain of Title and Rights Clearance

Chain of title records how the production acquired the rights it needs. Rights clearance checks material used in the finished work. Together they support financing, distribution and [[wiki/concepts/production-insurance|Errors and Omissions Insurance]].

## Build the Rights Record

Track each asset, owner, agreement, payment, permitted use, territory, media, term and outstanding condition. Include underlying literary material, screenplay contributions, producer transfers, cast and contributor releases, music, archive footage, photographs, artwork and trademarks where relevant. Link the executed document and evidence that required payments or option exercises occurred.

An option reserves an opportunity to acquire specified rights for a defined period. Calendar its expiry, extension conditions and exercise price against production and delivery. An expired option can break the financing timetable even when the script and budget are ready. [WIPO’s rights-clearance guide](https://www.wipo.int/web-publications/rights-clearance-a-guide-for-independent-filmmakers/en/2-rights-clearance-from-idea-to-distribution.html) explains the clearance process from development through distribution.

## Match Rights to the Intended Business

| Planned Use | Rights Question |
| --- | --- |
| Film or television release | Does the production control the adaptation and exploitation rights for the intended markets? |
| Music in the film and trailer | Do the relevant composition and recording permissions cover each use, term and territory? |
| Archive or third-party footage | Does the license cover the edit, distribution channels and promotion? |
| Sequel, remake or spin-off | Who owns or controls those rights, and what approvals or further payments apply? |
| Games and merchandise | Did the production retain these categories, character uses and likeness permissions? |

## Keep the Schedule Current

Update the clearance record when scripts, footage or music change. Reconcile it with delivery requirements and insurer review. Record unresolved items with an owner, cost and decision date: acquire, replace, remove or obtain a documented legal assessment of the intended use.

## Analysis and Inference

Use one rights-availability schedule across [[wiki/concepts/distribution-economics|Sales]], [[wiki/concepts/gap-collateral|Gap Valuation]] and [[wiki/concepts/ip-monetization-beyond-screen|IP Licensing]]. An asset cannot support two incompatible exclusive grants. A lender’s [[wiki/concepts/security-package|Security Interest]] does not repair missing ownership. Review option expiry, territorial restrictions and consent requirements before assigning value to unsold rights.
