# Monetizing IP Beyond the Screen

A production can generate revenue from retained rights beyond film and television distribution: games, merchandise, publishing, live experiences and other licensed uses. Start with the [[wiki/concepts/chain-of-title-and-rights|Rights Schedule]], then evaluate each opportunity as a separate commercial agreement.

## Establish What Can Be Licensed

Identify characters, artwork, trademarks, story elements and other assets within the grant. Check underlying author rights, talent likeness approvals, music restrictions, existing exclusivity and distributor reservations. Define product category, media, territory, term, approval process and sublicensing authority. Permission to distribute a film does not automatically include every use of its characters.

## Build the License Economics

| Item | Model Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Advance or minimum guarantee | Record the binding payment obligation, conditions and recoupment against royalties |
| Royalty | Define the sales base, exclusions, rate, returns and reporting period |
| Development and activation costs | Assign responsibility for design, game production, marketing and launch |
| Platform or agent fees | Apply to the correct base and identify who pays |
| Approval and delivery | Track asset delivery, product approvals and launch deadlines |
| Audit and termination | Preserve sales records, underpayment remedies, sell-off terms and rights reversion |

Separate royalty forecasts from signed minimum payments. Do not count an advance and the royalties that recoup it twice. Connect any assigned receivable to [[wiki/concepts/security-package|Security]] and the agreed collection route.

## Spaceport and Adjacent Partners

[Spaceport’s IP-holder offering](https://www.spaceport.xyz/solutions-ip) connects IP owners and developers through its Portal and supports interactive assets and activations. Its [print-on-demand service](https://www.spaceport.xyz/pod) describes licensed products and partner-led production and fulfillment. For a producer, this is an avenue to explore interactive and merchandise exploitation of available rights.

[Negosh](https://negosh.com/) connects brands and product creators for licensing across product categories, territories and channels. The [[wiki/syntheses/finance-and-ip-directory|Directory]] separates these commercial partners from lenders, equity investors and grants.

## Analysis and Inference

Assess the contribution after partner fees, production costs, approvals and the collection timetable. Measure contracted revenue separately from audience engagement. A marketing activation may cost money without creating a minimum payment to the producer. Include only binding, available cash in the production financing plan; carry speculative royalties in a labeled exploitation forecast.
