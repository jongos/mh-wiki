# Television and Streaming Finance

The financing structure of a television series or streaming project follows its contract. Identify who owns the production, who covers its cost and who controls future exploitation. The buyer’s brand does not establish those terms.

## Compare the Deal Structures

| Structure | Commercial Arrangement | Financing Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commissioned production | Buyer funds agreed production activity under a commission | Do milestones cover costs before they fall due, and who carries overages? |
| License or pre-buy | Buyer pays for specified rights, territory and term | What rights remain available and what delivery conditions trigger payment? |
| Deficit financing | Initial license covers less than the full production cost | Which committed sources fund the balance and which retained rights support recoupment? |
| Cost-plus arrangement | Contract funds defined costs plus agreed compensation | What counts as cost, what approvals apply, and what participation or rights remain? |
| Production services | Company produces for a rights owner for agreed compensation | Who employs the crew, owns assets, claims incentives and bears completion obligations? |

[WIPO’s film and television guide](https://www.wipo.int/publications/en/details.jsp?id=4611&plang=EN), chapter 2, explains the relationship between television rights, deficit financing and cost-plus arrangements. These are structural categories; use the executed agreement for a particular buyer.

## Finance the Actual Payment Schedule

Map signature, commencement, episodic delivery, acceptance and final payment. Separate a commissioned budget from cash drawable today. If payment follows accepted delivery, identify the working-capital source and assignments required for a [[wiki/concepts/pre-sales-collateral|Receivables Loan]].

For a series, reconcile season and episode budgets, delivery batches and approval gates. Do not count an unexercised renewal or later season as committed repayment. Track rights options, extension periods and exclusivity that affect the producer’s ability to sell elsewhere.

## Allocate Continuing Obligations

Specify responsibility for guild residuals, participations, music renewals, delivery materials, insurance, storage, reporting and audit. Include retained [[wiki/concepts/ip-monetization-beyond-screen|Ancillary Rights]] only where the commission and underlying rights permit their exploitation. Future royalties belong in a separate forecast from fixed license installments.

## Analysis and Inference

A well-funded buyer can still create a production cash shortfall through late milestones, acceptance disputes or unbudgeted changes. Test the same [[wiki/concepts/full-financing|Completion Sufficiency]] and [[wiki/concepts/cash-control-and-waterfalls|Cash Controls]] used for film lending. Keep the episode-based §168(k) eligibility rules separate from the season definition in the [[wiki/concepts/proposed-federal-film-tv-credit|Proposed Federal Credit]].
