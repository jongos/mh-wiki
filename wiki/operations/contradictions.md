## Scope tensions to monitor

These are not current contradictions, but they could produce inconsistent future pages if left implicit:

| Topic | Position A | Position B | Maintenance rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Policy thresholds | Briefs state numeric MediaHedge limits as current policy. | The briefs provide no effective date, version or approval authority. | Preserve the figures as source-backed internal policy; mark current application for verification. |
| Completion protection | Completion and delivery preserve multiple repayment paths. | Completion protection does not insure obligor solvency, tax-credit realization or commercial value. | Never describe completion coverage as a repayment guaranty. |
| Completion and surety | Completion protection addresses production and delivery. | Surety may address a specifically bonded payment or performance obligation after its trigger. | Map the obligation, provider, trigger, remedy, claim process and priority separately; never combine them as generic insurance. |
| Pre-sales and gap | An executed minimum guarantee can create a contractual receivable after delivery and acceptance. | A sales estimate for unsold rights is not a receivable or payment obligation. | Keep contractual receivable eligibility separate from market-dependent gap valuation and concentration. |
| Gap pricing | The gap brief states that gap inclusion generally contributes approximately three to five percentage points to blended pricing. | The brief gives no policy version or effective date, and pricing does not establish realized return. | Mark the statement `needs-review`; verify current pricing authority and loan-tape results before use. |
| Cash control | CAMA and payment directions route and allocate proceeds. | Article 9 control, bank onboarding and reconciliation remain separate requirements. | Map each function to its specific document and operating evidence. |
| Higher pricing in stress | Extension/default economics may increase contractual amounts. | Delay, nonaccrual, cost and loss can reduce XIRR and net return. | Report cash realization and risk-adjusted results, not nominal rate alone. |
| Diversified collateral | A loan may include several collateral categories. | Those categories can share completion, delivery or obligor dependencies. | Test common failure drivers before giving diversification credit. |
