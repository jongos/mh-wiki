Sundance Institute Documentary Fund
Reviewed URLs:
https://www.sundance.org/programs/documentary-film/
Observed scope: Independent documentary support; follow the fund’s current stage and eligibility rules.
Public contact or route: dfp@sundance.org; use the program’s application route rather than email as an application.

SFFILM Rainin Grant
Reviewed URLs:
https://sffilm.org/rainin-grant/
Observed scope: Narrative features addressing social justice with a professional/economic benefit to the Bay Area filmmaking community.
Public contact or route: grants@sffilm.org; program page and application instructions. The page says the 2026 round is closed.

Catapult Film Fund
Reviewed URLs:
https://catapultfilmfund.org/
Observed scope: Early-stage documentary research/development grants and mentorship.
Public contact or route: info@catapultfilmfund.org; How to Apply for rounds and deadlines.

Austin Film Society
Reviewed URLs:
https://www.austinfilm.org/afs-grants/afs-grants-overview/
Observed scope: Grants for Texas-based filmmakers, including feature and short-film routes.
Public contact or route: filmmakersupport@austinfilm.org. Page says feature applications reopen in spring 2027 and shorts in summer 2027; current rounds closed.

ITVS
Reviewed URLs:
https://itvs.org/get-funded/
https://itvs.org/contact/
Observed scope: Documentary development and coproduction for public media; rights and delivery obligations require separate review.
Public contact or route: itvs@itvs.org; +1 415-356-8383 for general organizational inquiries. Apply through Get Funded, not the general inbox.

British Film Institute
Reviewed URLs:
https://www.bfi.org.uk/get-funding-support
https://www.bfi.org.uk/get-funding-support/meet-bfi-filmmaking-fund-team
Observed scope: UK development, production and other screen support, including international coproduction routes; some support carries recoupment.
Public contact or route: Official Get Funding and Support portal and its program-specific support/team routes.

Telefilm Canada
Reviewed URLs:
https://telefilm.ca/en/programmes/production-program
Observed scope: Production and post-production financing for eligible Canadian feature-film companies; separate documentary program.
Public contact or route: Production Program page’s Contact Us section and Dialogue application route; select the appropriate regional/program contact.

IDFA Bertha Fund
Reviewed URLs:
https://professionals.idfa.nl/en/training-funding/funding/about-the-idfa-bertha-fund/
Observed scope: Documentary support for filmmakers in eligible countries across Africa, Asia, Eastern Europe, Latin America, the Caribbean and Oceania; separate European minority-coproduction route.
Public contact or route: Use the fund’s category-specific Learn More/application routes and IDFA Contact link. Check the applicable country list.
