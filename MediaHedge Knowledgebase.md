# Welcome to the MediaHedge Knowledgebase

Come on in. This wiki is a guided map of how MediaHedge thinks about media credit: how a production becomes financeable, how collateral builds the borrowing base, how cash is captured, how risk changes after closing, and how a financing partner measures returns.

You do not need to read it front to back. Start with the explanation that matches your questions, follow the internal links, and keep drilling until your questions are answered or you feel you need to speak to our team again.

> [!tip] Financier? Start here
> Open the [[wiki/syntheses/financier-diligence-route|Financier Diligence Guide]] for a decision-first route through underwriting, controls, downside protection, portfolio construction and realized return.

> [!warning] Evidence boundary
> The wiki explains the source-stated MediaHedge framework. Current policy authority, transaction-specific legal conclusions and realized portfolio performance still require verification; see [[wiki/operations/research-backlog|Open Diligence Questions]].

## Financier quick start

1. Read the [[wiki/syntheses/financier-diligence-route|Financier Diligence Guide]] to frame the investment questions.
2. Compare value sources in the [[wiki/syntheses/repayment-and-risk-map|Repayment and Risk Map]].
3. Inspect the [[wiki/syntheses/policy-rails-and-control-matrix|Policy Rails and Control Matrix]], including its freshness warning.
4. Follow the [[wiki/syntheses/credit-lifecycle|Credit Lifecycle]] from screen through recovery.
5. Finish with [[wiki/concepts/portfolio-construction|Portfolio Construction]] and [[wiki/concepts/financier-return-economics|Return Economics]].
